Terms of Use

Last updated 13 August 2026.

1. What Litera is

Litera is a free, non-commercial website that publishes public-domain Georgian literary texts together with modern-Georgian renderings and word glosses. It is run by an individual, not a company. There is nothing for sale: no subscriptions, no paid tiers, no adverts.

2. Using the site

You may read, quote and cite anything here for study, teaching or research. Please do not scrape the site at a rate that degrades it for other readers, attempt to break into it, or send anything through the contact form that you do not have the right to send.

3. Accounts

An account is optional — nothing on the site is behind a login today. If you create one, keep your password to yourself. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes and cannot be read back. A forgotten password can be reset from the login page via a one-hour email link. Email us and an account can be deleted with flask delete-user.

4. Copyright and attribution

The original works are in the public domain. Individual transcriptions are reproduced from published digital editions under their own licences, and each reader page prints its source, revision and licence at the foot of the text. Modern renderings and glosses written for this site may be reused for non-commercial educational purposes with attribution to Litera.

If you hold rights in something published here and believe it should not be, write to us and it will be taken down while the claim is checked.

5. Accuracy

The texts are transcriptions and the modern renderings are working translations, marked in the database as draft, reviewed or final — a status the reader displays rather than hides. Errors exist. Litera is a study aid, not an authoritative critical edition, and it is not a substitute for your syllabus or your teacher. Do not rely on it as your only source in an examination.
