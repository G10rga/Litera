Tariel

"I, Tariel, the panther-clad, wanderer of the desert, seeking the lost sun of my existence."

The central figure of Shota Rustaveli's 'The Knight in the Panther's Skin'. Embodying the concept of 'mijnuruba' (courtly love), Tariel's journey is one of profound emotional depth and chivalric excellence.
