Our Mission: Bridging Tradition and Technology

Litera was born from a simple realization: the beauty of Georgian literature shouldn't be lost in the complexity of archaic language.

THE ORIGIN

Built for Students, by Students

We remember the late nights before the ეროვნული გამოცდები (National Exams), wrestling with the dense stanzas of The Knight in the Panther's Skin or the complex theology of hagiographic martyrdoms. Litera was created for those who find classical Georgian texts challenging, turning dense, exam-critical content into a clear, navigable path.

Our platform serves as a modern bridge, preserving the sanctity of the original text while providing the high-fidelity tools necessary for academic success in the 21st century.

"Preserving the soul of the script."
