Bridging tradition and readable Georgian

Georgian schoolchildren are asked to read texts written in a language centuries away from the one they speak. Litera keeps the original on the page and puts help beside it — not instead of it.

THE ORIGIN

Built for students who need the text, not a substitute

The usual remedy for hard Georgian is a summary that replaces the text. Litera takes the opposite approach: keep the original, put a modern rendering next to it, and explain the hard words where they occur.

Litera is an independent, non-commercial project. The code is open on GitHub. Corrections to a transcription or a gloss are the most useful thing you can send.

"Preserving the soul of the script."
