Common questions

Do I need an account to read? expand_more
No. Every text, rendering, and gloss on Litera is readable without logging in. Accounts are optional and reserved for features still being built.
Where do the texts come from? expand_more
Original texts are public-domain transcriptions imported from published digital editions. Each reader page prints its source URL, revision, and licence at the bottom.
