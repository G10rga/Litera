What is on the site

Four things, all of them backed by real text in the database.

menu_book

ვეფხისტყაოსანი

Chapter by chapter. Original quatrains on the left, the modern rendering on the right, archaic words underlined and explained on hover.

შუშანიკის წამება

The oldest surviving work of Georgian literature, in twenty sections, with the same two-column reader and a glossary from NPLG.

library_books

The library

Every other work that has been imported, with its source, revision and licence recorded, and an honest percentage of how much has been modernised so far.

format_quote

Aphorisms

The collected aphorisms, numbered, for quoting in an essay without hunting for the line.

Two columns

The original is never rewritten or replaced. The modern rendering sits beside it so you can check one against the other.

Glosses in place

Archaic words are marked in the text itself. Hover or focus one and you get the reading for that passage, not a generic dictionary entry.

Sources shown

Each reader page prints where its text came from, which revision, and under what licence. Chapters that have not been prepared say so instead of showing a placeholder.

Where the project is

Litera is a work in progress, maintained by one person. The two readers above work; the library grows as texts are imported and modernised. There is no exam-question bank, no essay generator and no AI tutor — if those arrive, they will appear here rather than being advertised in advance.

Found a transcription error or a wrong gloss? Tell me and it gets fixed at the source.
