Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 August 2026.

1. Summary

Litera collects as little as it can. There is no analytics script, no advertising, no tracking pixels and no third-party cookies. Data is collected in exactly two places: when you register an account, and when you send a message through the contact form.

2. What is stored

If you register: your name, your email address, an optional school year or grade, a salted hash of your password (never the password itself), and the date you signed up. This is used only to identify you when you log in.

If you use the contact form: the name, email address, subject and message you type, plus the time you sent it. This is stored so a person can read and reply to it.

Cookies: one session cookie to keep you logged in, and a second longer-lived cookie only if you tick “stay logged in”. Both are strictly functional. A CSRF token is also held in the session to protect form submissions.

Server logs: the hosting provider records ordinary web-server request logs, which include IP addresses. These are not used to build profiles and are not combined with account data.

3. What is not done

Your data is not sold, rented, shared with advertisers, or used to train anything. There is no newsletter and no marketing email. Nothing you read on the site is tracked against your account.

4. Where it is stored

In the application database on the server that runs the site, reachable only by the maintainer. Traffic to the site is served over HTTPS.

5. How long it is kept

Account records are kept until you ask for the account to be deleted. Contact messages are kept until the matter is dealt with, and are cleared out periodically after that.

6. Your requests

Email {{ config.CONTACT_EMAIL }} to ask for a copy of what is held about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. Requests are handled by hand by one person, so allow a few days; there is no automated self-service tool for this yet and this policy does not pretend otherwise.

7. Children

The site is aimed at secondary-school students and above. No account is required to read anything, and accounts should not be created by children under 13.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. See also the terms of use.
