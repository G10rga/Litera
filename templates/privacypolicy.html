Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 24, 2024

Welcome to Litera Study Platform. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This policy outlines how we handle the scholarly journey you undertake with us, ensuring your data remains as secure as a locked archival collection.

Data Collection

In our pursuit of academic excellence, we collect information that you provide to us directly. This includes the following categories of scholarly data:

account_circle

Personal Identity

Name, email address, and institutional affiliation used to personalize your study dashboard.

history_edu

Academic Progress

Completed modules, quiz scores, and saved character analyses to track your curriculum mastery.

We do not collect sensitive biological or biometric data. Your intellectual growth is the only metric we track.

Cookies and Tracking

Litera uses "Cookies" to enhance your library experience. These small text files act as digital bookmarks, allowing us to:

  • Remember your preferred font sizes for dense literary analysis.
  • Keep you authenticated during long study sessions.
  • Analyze which study guides are most effective for exam preparation.

You may disable cookies in your browser settings, though some archival features may become unavailable.

Use of Information

Your data is processed exclusively for the purpose of facilitating your education. We use it to:

check_circle
Provide Services: Ensuring your access to modern translations and hagiographic summaries is seamless.
check_circle
Improve Experience: Using anonymized data to refine our character relationship diagrams and study chips.
check_circle
Safety: Protecting our platform from intellectual property theft and malicious actors.

Data Sharing

We believe in the sanctity of private study. We do NOT sell your data to third-party marketers. We only share information with:

  • Service Providers: Cloud hosting partners who maintain our digital archives.
  • Legal Obligations: If required by law to protect our rights or comply with judicial proceedings.
  • Academic Partners: Only with your explicit consent for collaborative research.

Your Academic Rights

You maintain full control over your digital footprint. At any time, you may request to:

Access Archive Correct Records Erase History Export Data

To exercise these rights, contact our Privacy Officer at privacy@litera-study.edu
